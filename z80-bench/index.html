Z80 Bench

A Z80 emulator for debugging programs and designing hardware around the Zilog Z80. It runs right in your browser, with nothing to install.

Open the emulator

Features

  • Pinout: a 40-pin view that updates every T-state. Click CLK, /INT, /NMI, /WAIT, /BUSREQ and /RESET to drive them by hand
  • Program input: Zilog-syntax assembler with labels and directives, or raw hex and Intel HEX. You can also import .asm, .hex or .bin files
  • Memory: 1 to 64 KB installed, editable in the memory view
  • Stepping: one T-state, one instruction, or run at 5 Hz, 50 Hz, 1 kHz or full speed, with breakpoints
  • Bus timing: waveforms of /M1, /MREQ, /IORQ, /RD, /WR and /RFSH for the current instruction
  • I/O: OUT writes shown as LED latches, and you set what IN returns for each port

Verified

Passes all 67 zexdoc groups, matches datasheet T-state counts, and assembles all 1,136 instruction forms correctly.

Coming next

Peripherals such as memory-mapped devices, UART and PIO.
