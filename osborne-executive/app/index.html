Clicking CLK pulses one clock. /INT is level-sensitive, /NMI triggers on a falling edge. Holding /WAIT low inserts wait states; /BUSREQ low floats the buses at the end of the current machine cycle; /RESET low holds the CPU in reset.

Wiring from the OCC2 schematic. The PIAs' E is the board's "68 ENABLE", high during an I/O cycle to that chip; R/W follows /WR; RS0 and RS1 are A0 and A1; CS0 and CS1 are tied high and /CS2 comes from the 74LS138 decoder. On UD12, port A drives the bank lines, CB1 is VBLANK and CB2 the 50/60 Hz select. The SIO's /TxC and /RxC come from the 8253 baud-rate timer, which is not emulated, so they stay idle. Click a highlighted input to drive it by hand.

Screen

Click the screen and type: your keyboard drives the Executive's key matrix through the ROM's own 60 Hz scan. Caps Lock toggles Alpha Lock, and Backspace acts as the ← key. The ROM is not included: load your own 8 KB image (execv12, CRC 70798C2F). Your browser keeps it, so you only load it once. The blue marker shows the electron beam while you step.

Program

Standard Zilog syntax, one instruction per line. Labels end with :; comments start with ;. Directives: ORG, EQU, DB, DW, DS, END. Numbers: 1Fh, 0x1F, $1F, %1010, 'A', and $ for the current address. Undocumented instructions (IXH, SLL, OUT (C),0) are accepted. Code starts at the Load at address; an ORG in the source overrides it. Import a file with the button or by dropping it on the editor.

Hex bytes separated by spaces or newlines. Text after ; is ignored. Write 0100: to continue loading at another address. Intel HEX files (lines starting with :) are also accepted and keep their own addresses. Raw binary ROM images (.bin, .rom) import here as hex.     Every breakpoint and stop appears here, including those set from the disassembly and registers; click one to jump to it. A memory stop halts after the instruction that changes the byte. A pin or bus stop halts on the clock where the pin changes or the bus takes the value, so while one is set, full speed runs clock by clock and is slower.
