Osborne Executive

An emulator of Osborne's 1983 portable, built on Z80 Bench and modelled from the OCC2 main logic board schematics. It boots the real ROM to its sign-on screen, with a working keyboard and amber display, and every chip's pins can be watched clock by clock.

Open the emulator Documentation

Features

  • The real ROM: load your own 8 KB Executive v1.2 ROM image (it isn't included); your browser remembers it. The ROM runs its self-test and shows its sign-on screen
  • Screen: the 80 × 24 amber display, drawn from the font the ROM loads, with half intensity, underline, blink, reverse video and the graphics character set
  • Keyboard: your keys drive the Executive's key matrix, scanned by the ROM 60 times a second
  • Memory banks: 128K of DRAM, the boot ROM, font RAM and video RAM switched by the system PIA, with memory and disassembly views that follow the active bank
  • Chips: live pinouts of the Z80A, both MC6821 PIAs and the Z80A SIO/2, wired as on the schematic
  • Memory chips: every memory chip with its pins live: the 2764 boot ROM, the 6116 scratch, video and font RAMs, and the sixteen 4164s of the RAM board. Click any pin to disconnect it or hold it high or low, and watch what the machine does with a stuck data bit, a dead address line or a chip fighting the bus
  • Real time: 3.99 MHz with 60 Hz video, or full speed
  • Debugging: all of Z80 Bench's tools, including stepping clock by clock, bus timing, breakpoints and register, memory, pin and bus stops
  • Fault finding: force any input pin high or low, or pull a chip off the bus altogether, and watch how the ROM copes

Coming next

The MB8877 floppy disk controller and disk images, so the Executive can boot CP/M Plus.
