SimpleMusic

Write your notes first, count the bars later. A simple music editor that runs right in your browser, with nothing to install.

Open the editor

How it works

  1. Write your melody. Pick a note length and click on the staff to place notes, without worrying about bars.
  2. Divide into bars. Choose the beats per bar and which note counts as one beat. Notes that cross a bar line are split and tied for you.

Features

  • Whole, half, quarter, eighth and sixteenth notes, plus dotted notes and rests
  • Sharps and flats, and drag a note up or down to change its pitch
  • Playback with an adjustable tempo from 40 to 200 bpm
  • Any time signature, with the option to fill the last bar with rests
  • Undo and redo, plus keyboard shortcuts for fast entry
  • Export your sheet as a PDF
  • Your work is remembered in your browser between visits

Source

Plain HTML, CSS and JavaScript, with no libraries or build step. Built with Claude Code.
