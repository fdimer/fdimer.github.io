Spectrum ULA Test Bench

A ZX Spectrum 48K whose ULA is the real logic, not a model of its behaviour. The Ferranti 6C001E-7 was transcribed gate for gate from Ferranti's own logic diagram and is simulated gate by gate, each gate with its delay. The Z80 runs on the clock the ULA puts out, so contention, the screen fetches, the interrupt and every dot of the picture come from those gates. It is for anyone who wants to help check the ULA; for using a Spectrum, the ZX Spectrum emulator is the one to open.

Open the test bench Documentation

Features

  • The gate-level ULA: the 6C001E-7 (mark 7) as a netlist of 385 cells (gates, flip-flops, counters, latches and pin drivers), simulated event by event; it runs at a few per cent of a real Spectrum's speed
  • Nothing modelled: the CPU clock and its holds, the lower RAM's RAS, CAS and write, the screen fetches, ROM select, port FE, the floating bus, INT and the colour lines all come from the ULA's pins
  • Your own ROM and snapshots: load a 16 KB Spectrum 48K ROM image (not included; your browser remembers it) and .sna or .z80 snapshots
  • Two starts: from a started machine (the ROM's start-up is run by the emulator and handed over), or from power-on
  • Picture check: every frame from the ULA's colour lines is compared, dot by dot, with the screen in its lower RAM
  • Meters and report: speed, frames, interrupts, T-states held and the program counter, and a summary of the run to paste into an issue or an email
  • Keyboard: your own, or the on-screen keys in the Spectrum's rows

How far it is checked

Against the emulator and Fuse's figures, the ULA's contention matches in every bus cycle tried, as do the floating bus, port FE, the screen fetches and every dot of the 256 × 192 picture. Two things stay open: the first dots of the left border, and where INT falls, which makes this ULA behave like a "late timing" 48K. Nothing has been compared with a real 6C001E-7 yet.

Not modelled

The tape, the speaker's sound, the colour encoder's analogue side (the picture is the colour lines that go into it), and the upper RAM's own chips.

Sources

Ferranti logic diagram LD6790/ULA6C001/1 revision 3 (the copy marked in red for mark 7) and the cell circuits 6C001/2, /3 and /4, from the Ferranti archive catalogued in 2026 by Happy Little Diodes (watch the video about the archive); the board from Timex's issue 6A circuit diagram. The netlist, the simulator and how each was checked are in the repository.
