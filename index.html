My Projects

Tools I've built for tinkering with hardware, music, maths, writing, fonts and the web. Pick a project to read more and grab the code.

Z80 Bench

A Z80 emulator for debugging and designing hardware, with a clickable pinout, hex and assembler input, configurable memory and step-by-step execution.

emulator · hardware

SimpleMusic

Write your notes first, count the bars later. A music editor that runs in your browser, with playback and PDF export.

music · editor

SimpleMusic Rhythm

A rhythm editor for beginners. Write a rhythm freely, pick a time signature, and it splits the rhythm into bars for you. With a metronome.

music · rhythm

TextBrowser

A text-only web browser with the look of a 1980s computer terminal. Pages show as glowing text and numbered links, with no pictures or video.

web · retro

Chain Calc

A calculator laid out like a tiny spreadsheet. Five cells, each with its own operation, and the running result shown at every step.

maths · calculator

SimpleLetters

A plain word processor: one A4 page, and you write on it. The only things you can change are the margins and the font size.

writing · calm

SimpleFunLetters

Write letters in your own handwriting. Draw any letter on squared paper and everything you type uses your drawing. For children 8 and up.

writing · kids

Font Developer

Design your own font in the browser. Draw each character with pixels, lines and arcs, watch the result as you go, and export a .ttf font.

fonts · design
