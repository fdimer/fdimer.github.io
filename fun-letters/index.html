SimpleFunLetters

Write letters in your own handwriting. Draw your own version of any letter, number or punctuation mark on squared paper, and everything you type afterwards uses your drawing. A calm letter writer for children aged 8 and up.

Features

  • Draw your own letters: pick a letter and draw it on squared paper, with the normal letter showing faintly behind as a guide. The right mouse button rubs out
  • Type with your drawings: press Done and everything you type from then on uses your version. Letters you haven't drawn use the normal font
  • Old writing keeps its look: redrawing a letter only changes new typing, so earlier text stays as it was
  • The same calm page as SimpleLetters: one A4 page, adjustable margins and text size, plus open, save, print, undo and redo
  • Remembers your letters: a new letter starts with your latest drawings
  • Private: collects no data, and letters and drawings stay on your computer

Windows app

SimpleFunLetters is a Windows app, coming soon to the Microsoft Store. Each time you press Done it builds a small font from your drawings, right on your computer, without installing anything in Windows. Letters are saved as .funletter files, and SimpleLetters' .sletter files open too.

Source
